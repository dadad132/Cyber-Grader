What the domain health check covers

{{ check_count }} checks across the five areas attackers and spam filters look at first. Every problem comes with a severity and a step-by-step fix your IT person or web host can follow.

Email security

SPF, DMARC, DKIM, MTA-STS, TLS reporting and mail server encryption decide whether criminals can send invoices "from" you, the scam behind most business email fraud.

Blacklists & reputation

Are your mail servers or your domain on Spamhaus, Barracuda, SpamCop, SURBL or URIBL? A listing quietly sends your emails to spam. Plus reverse DNS for your mail servers.

SSL certificate & TLS

Is the certificate trusted and renewing on time? Are old, broken TLS versions switched off? Does every visitor land on HTTPS?

Security headers

HSTS, Content-Security-Policy, clickjacking and cookie protection: the browser-side defences that stop script injection and session theft.

DNS security

DNSSEC and CAA protect against forged DNS answers and rogue certificates, plus a published contact for security researchers.
